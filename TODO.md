# HOUZZ STUDIOS Website — Outcomes

## [x] 1. Art direction, shared shell, and responsive behavior

The website feels like a high-end architecture/interior design studio combined with a premium furniture house: uncommon, sophisticated, artistic, intentional, calm, minimal but visually rich, with refined warm architectural visual language, luxury-editorial typography, large photography, generous whitespace, asymmetrical magazine layouts, subtle interactions and cinematic transitions. Use warm ivory/off-white, soft stone, sand, natural wood, charcoal/almost-black and subtle muted brown. Do not use bright blue, purple gradients, neon, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, generic SaaS layouts, huge repetitive buttons, floating blobs, excessive shadows, random gradients, generic stock-photo collages, or the same repeated card grid. Do not repeat “heading + paragraph + 3 cards”, generic hero/card/three-column/icon-feature/testimonial/centered-heading/CTA patterns. Alternate full-bleed photos, asymmetric image/text, horizontal editorial layouts, vertical columns, overlapping images, split screens, large typography, image-led navigation, magazine grids, dark cinematic sections, text-only moments, horizontal scrolling, full-width photography and architectural whitespace. Every major section has its own visual composition. Desktop is highly editorial; tablet preserves composition rather than simply shrinking it; mobile is deliberately redesigned, preserves hierarchy and generous spacing, uses horizontal image scrolling where useful, has elegant typography, proper mobile navigation and comfortable touch targets. Animations are subtle and premium: smooth image reveals, slow parallax, text fade/slide, image scale on hover, horizontal scroll galleries, sticky storytelling, subtle cursor interactions and smooth page transitions; avoid bouncing, huge text animation, constant floating elements, flashy effects and excessive scroll-triggered animation.

The header is transparent over the hero, with HOUZZ / STUDIOS on the left; centered Home, About, Furniture, Interior Design, Projects and Contact navigation; Search, Bag and Menu icons on the right; and an ivory/white sticky state on scroll. Use elegant serif major headlines and clean modern sans-serif body/navigation. Use semantic HTML, accessible controls, meaningful link/button labels, visible focus, comfortable touch targets and optimized distinct imagery.

## [x] 3. Secondary pages and working interactions

Create these page types: Home; About; Furniture; Furniture Category/Product Detail; Interior Design; Projects; Project Detail; Contact. About is a long editorial studio story with large photography, timeline/storytelling, craftsmanship, design philosophy, materials, statistics and studio imagery, not simply text sections stacked vertically. Furniture is a premium catalog with Sofas, Chairs, Tables, Dining, Bedroom and Storage categories; it uses an editorial product layout instead of a basic ecommerce grid, with minimal elegant filtering. Category/product detail uses large immersive product photography and gallery; name, description, materials, dimensions, price and enquiry/add-to-cart action; separate “Materials & Craft”, “Dimensions”, “Care” and “Shipping” information. Interior Design presents a premium studio service with Hero, design philosophy, process, Residential, Commercial, Materials, Selected work and consultation CTA; process is a visual timeline, not ordinary cards. Projects is an editorial archive with varied image sizes/compositions and no uniform three-column grid. Project Detail is an immersive case study with project title, location, year, type, large hero, design concept, gallery, materials, before/after where appropriate, final photography and related projects. Contact is a minimal luxury page with “Let’s create something beautiful.”, Name, Email, Phone, Project Type, Budget and Message fields, and studio/contact information.

Make the header/footer and all internal links functional. Search, bag and menu controls open usable interfaces. Filters actually filter categories/products. Product add-to-bag updates bag state and supports enquiry; do not imply real checkout or payment processing. Contact fields are labeled and validated, with accessible inline error/success feedback. Do not claim that the form sends, stores or transmits data without a configured service. Never invent verified brand contact details; keep any absent address/phone/email/product/project facts explicitly replaceable.
